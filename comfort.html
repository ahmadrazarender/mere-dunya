The side you bring out

My comfort zone

Main normally apni responsibilities ko seriously leta hoon. Koshish karta hoon ke mature rahun aur har cheez samajh kar handle karun.

Lekin aap ke saath kabhi kabhi main bilkul bacha ban jata hoon. Choti choti zid, mazaak, random baatein — kyun ke aap ke saamne mujhe har waqt strong ya serious banne ki zaroorat mehsoos nahi hoti.

Aap ke saath main apna real self reh sakta hoon. Mere liye yeh comfort bohat badi baat hai. Aur main chahta hoon ke aap bhi mere saath bilkul apni tarah reh sakein.
