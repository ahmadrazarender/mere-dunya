From my heart, in simple words

A little letter

Meri pyari Gull,

Main aap ko shayad har baar bata nahi pata ke aap mere liye kitni important hain. Kabhi kabhi aap ki narazgi se bhi dar lagta hai, kyun ke main nahi chahta ke meri kisi baat se aap ka dil kharab ho.

Main chahta hoon ke aap ko meri taraf se time, attention aur care milti rahe. Aap jo baat share karti hain, main usay dhyan se sunna chahta hoon. Aap ki choti choti baatein bhi mere liye matter karti hain.

Main perfect nahi hoon. Kabhi meri koi baat buri lag jaye to mujhe bata dijiyega. Main samajhna chahta hoon, sirf apni baat manwana nahi.

Bas itna kehna tha ke aap meri life ka bohat pyara hissa hain. Aap ke saath guzara hua waqt mere liye genuinely special hai.

Aap ka Rumi ♥
